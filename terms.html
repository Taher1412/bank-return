Terms

Tally is a personal, non-commercial app, used only by the person who made it. It is not offered to the public and there is no account to sign up for.

Read only

Tally reads account balances and transactions to show where money goes. It cannot make payments, move money, or change anything at a bank.

Bank access

Bank access goes through Enable Banking and is subject to Enable Banking's terms and to your bank's. You approve it at your bank, and you can end it at any time.

No warranty

Tally is provided as is. Figures shown in it are for personal budgeting; your bank's own records are the reference.
